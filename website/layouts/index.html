One app, two directions

Understand the room. Join the conversation.

Listen Mode translates the audio around you. Speak Mode turns your voice into spoken translation for calls and conversations.

Capture system audio directly

Translate meetings, videos, and calls from system audio or a microphone. Translito uses ScreenCaptureKit on macOS and loopback capture on Windows.

System audioMicrophonesVirtual devices

Private by design

Speech recognition and translation run locally after the required models download—using native Apple frameworks on macOS and CPU-friendly models on Windows.

Speak in another language

Speak Mode transcribes your voice, translates it, and plays the result through your chosen output—including BlackHole or VB-Audio Cable for calls.

Always within reach

Start, stop, switch modes, and open Settings from the macOS menu bar or Windows notification area. Global hotkeys work everywhere.

Languages that stay flexible

Choose source and target languages independently for listening and speaking. Language packs or translation models download when needed.

Every translation, saved

Keep timestamped source text and translations, choose your transcript folder, and jump straight to saved sessions in Finder or Explorer.
