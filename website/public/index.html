One app, two directions

Understand the room. Join the conversation.

Listen Mode translates the audio around you. Speak Mode turns your voice into spoken translation for calls and conversations.

Capture system audio directly

Translate meetings, videos, and calls without virtual cables. Translito uses macOS ScreenCaptureKit for clean, direct system-audio capture.

System audioMicrophonesVirtual devices

Private by design

Speech recognition runs locally with WhisperKit. Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework.

Speak in another language

Speak Mode transcribes your voice, translates it, and plays the result through your chosen output—including BlackHole for Slack or Zoom.

Always within reach

Start, stop, switch modes, and open Settings from the menu bar. Add global hotkeys and launch Translito automatically at login.

Languages that stay flexible

Choose source and target languages independently for listening and speaking. macOS downloads language packs when needed.

Every translation, saved

Keep timestamped source text and translations, choose your transcript folder, and jump straight to saved sessions in Finder.
